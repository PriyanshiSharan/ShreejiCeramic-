CARE & MAINTENANCE

To maintain the high-quality finish and operation of your Aquant products, strict adherence to these maintenance protocols is essential. Modern cleaning agents contain a variety of chemicals; only by following the instructions below can you prevent permanent damage.

Cleaning Equipment

Use a soft, damp cloth or a high-quality micro-fiber cloth. Never use abrasive sponges or scouring agents which cause permanent scratches.

Cleaning Process

Apply cleaning agents onto a soft cloth first. Never spray directly onto the product as liquid can enter gaps & openings causing internal damage.

Tolietries Residue

Residues of liquid soaps, shampoos, hair dyes, perfumes, and lipstick can cause damage. Rinse thoroughly with clean water after use.

Testing & Timing

Always test any cleaning agent on an inconspicuous area first. Do not allow the cleaner to remain on the product for an extended period.

Chemical Restrictions

Aquant finishes are premium coatings. The following substances will cause irreversible damage:
  • Hydrochloric Acid
  • Formic Acid
  • Phosphorus Acid
  • Chlorine / Bleach
  • Acetic Acid
WARNING: Never mix different cleaning agents. After cleaning, always dry the product surface with a soft, dry cloth to prevent water spots and mineral buildup.
